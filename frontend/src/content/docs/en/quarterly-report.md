# Quarterly report

The quarterly report is a PowerPoint (PPTX) presentation ready to take to the client: the quarter's numbers, the comparison with earlier periods, an analysis and suggested actions.

## What it contains

The slides, in order:

1. Cover, with the quarter, countries and filters used.
2. Key indicators: revenue, units sold, ad spend, ad sales, ROAS, ACoS, TACOS, return rate. Each is compared with the **previous quarter** and with the **same quarter last year**.
3. Monthly revenue trend, with a chart.
4. Best and worst ASINs: the top 10 by revenue and the biggest drops.
5. Catalogue health: the distribution of Listing quality traffic lights and the open anomalies (Content protection and Variations).
6. Search terms from Brand Analytics, one slide per country.
7. Tracked competitors: price and BSR.
8. Insights and actions.
9. Data notes: what is missing and what the sources are.

A section with no data still gets its slide, with the message "No data available".

## Generating it

From **Performance**, in the **Quarterly report** card: choose year, quarter, language (Italian or English) and at least one account; each account is a country. The optional filters are **Brand**, **Category** and a list of **ASINs** (up to 500, one per line or comma-separated). **Generate quarterly report** downloads the file. The Dashboard now has brand and category filters too.

## Scheduling it

In the scheduled reports pick the **Quarterly** frequency: the report goes out on the first day of each quarter at the time you set and covers the quarter just ended, for the chosen accounts or all of them. The quarterly report cannot be combined with other reports. Sending by e-mail follows the same rules as the other scheduled reports (see Email reports).

## Actual or estimate

Every figure carries a source tag: **Actual** (Amazon reports: Vendor Central, Seller Central, Amazon Ads, Brand Analytics) or **Estimate** (forecasts, Helium 10). For now all the report's numbers are actuals. If the quarter is not over yet, the slide says so and uses the data up to today.

## AI insights

The insights are written by artificial intelligence when the service is available. When credits have run out or the service does not answer, the report **does not stop**: it uses insights generated by rules on the data (falling revenue, red listings, open anomalies, declining ASINs, TACOS, competitors, search terms, returns). The slide's subtitle says which of the two was used.

## Data that can be missing

- **Vendor** accounts have no Brand Analytics search terms.
- Ad spend cannot be computed with a brand or category filter.
- Amounts from countries with different currencies are summed without conversion to euro.
- Advertising data starts on 28/04/2026 (see Known limits).

Every gap is listed in the **Data notes**.
