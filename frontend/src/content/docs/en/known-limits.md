# Known limits

Some things are not there, or are only partly there. These are not faults: they are Amazon's limits or deliberate scope choices. Reading this page before opening a support request saves you time, and gives you a ready answer when the client is the one asking.

## Advertising data starts on 28/04/2026

Before that date, campaign data **does not exist** in the platform, and it cannot be recovered.

The reason is an Amazon limit: it only makes the last ~95 days of **Sponsored Products** available. When the historical load ran, 28/04/2026 was as far back as it could reach — and that boundary never moves again.

There is a detail that causes plenty of needless alarm: the other two campaign types have even shorter windows. **Sponsored Brands** keeps around 60 days and **Sponsored Display** around 65. So those two start **later** than 28/04.

Practical consequence: any "collapse" in advertising spend or sales that you see **before June** is almost always **missing data**, not worse performance. Before writing it into a report, check that the period is covered.

## Competitor reviews and ratings

On competitor product cards, the **star rating** and the **number of reviews** show up in most cases as **N/A**.

This is not data missing by mistake: Amazon does not provide this information for third-party products. There is no setting that unlocks it.

What normally does stay available is competitors' **price** and **sales rank**, which are the two indicators you can actually act on.

## Catalog image management

You cannot upload, edit or manage product images from Inthezon.

It would require a dedicated external file store, which is not part of the platform's current scope. Images are managed directly on Seller Central or Vendor Central.

## The artificial intelligence features

Some parts of the platform are generated by an external artificial intelligence service that runs on **credits**:

- the strategic recommendations;
- the market analysis;
- the written text of the brand analysis.

When the credits run out, **only these sections** stop producing new content. You still see what was already generated, but nothing new is added.

Everything else — sales, orders, campaigns, catalog, exports — keeps working normally. If you notice that only these three sections have stopped while the numbers keep updating, credits are the right suspicion, not a fault.

## How much history is available

It depends on the account type:

| Account type | Sales history |
| --- | --- |
| Seller Central | up to **24 months** |
| Vendor Central | beyond 24 months |

The 24-month cap on Seller accounts is imposed by Amazon: further back than that, the data is simply not delivered. Vendor accounts reach further.

Keep it in mind when preparing year-on-year comparisons: on a recently connected Seller account, the comparison period may not exist at all.
